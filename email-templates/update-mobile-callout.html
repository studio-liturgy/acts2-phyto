An update on where phyto is at, and a call for help building the mobile app.

phyto is growing in ways I never expected and I could use your help getting it onto mobile!

The number of accounts made and the number of people following along on Instagram have both more than quadrupled! And in the past couple of weeks, I’ve had zero technical bug reports, which is honestly a miracle for a one-man band that doesn’t have a technical background.

What’s meant the most, though, are the messages. People from all over the world writing in to encourage me to keep going, telling me how phyto is blessing their community, and sharing what it looks like in their own context. Seeing song sets pop up in languages I can’t even read, from gatherings I’ll never see in person, warms my heart every time.

Whether you use phyto every week or just tried it once, thank you for being a piece of the pie!

Now that the app is stable, phyto will be focussed on developing a mobile app! There have been many of you reaching out desiring to help out, so here’s your chance! I’m open to help in other areas too (backend, design, social media, and more), but right now I’m specifically focused on app development. If you love Jesus, have experience with mobile development (React Native, iOS, Android, or similar), and want to contribute to phyto’s next chapter, I’d genuinely love to hear from you.

I’ve been intentional in the way I’ve been building phyto so far, and by being a part, you would be invited to walk with the same integrity (and hopefully we can build a team together and have some fun!).

Fill out the form below and I’ll follow up.
